Period Tracker Information

A simple calendar-based estimate of your next cycle.

This is a general calendar estimate, not a medical diagnosis. Cycles vary naturally — if yours is irregular or you have concerns, speak with a doctor or gynaecologist.

Pregnancy Due Date Calculator

Estimated due date using Naegele's rule.

Enter a date to estimate your due date, current week of pregnancy, and trimester.

Only around 5% of babies are born exactly on their due date — this is an estimate. Please confirm dates and progress with your doctor or midwife.
